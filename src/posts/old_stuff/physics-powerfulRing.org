#+TITLE: Ode to the Standard Model

Six for the strong force, particles confined quarks: down and up,
strange and charmed, bottom and top. Six for the weak force, all to
itself assigned leptons: electron, muon, tau and their neutrinos.

Force carriers to travel far and in the darkness, find them. A higgs
boson to hold them back, give them mass, and discind them.

One theory to rule them all, and with symmetry, bind them. In the realm
where the particles lay, born with the big bang behind them.

By now you might have realized that this poem was inspired by
[[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Ring][the one ring]].

A higgs boson to give them mass and who's the boss, remind them. and as
massive particles, brand them. to take control, and mind them. a bane
for mankind

One theory to rule them all, one theory to find them. One theory to
bring them all, and with the power of physics, bind them.
